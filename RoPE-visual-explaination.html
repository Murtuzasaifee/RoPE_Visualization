08 · Why it worksRoPE · Explained

The proof fits on a napkin.Two rules about turning do all the work.

m = 0 · n = 2
1
Turn the query by its position, the key by its position.
q′ = R(mθ)·q k′ = R(nθ)·k
2
Attention score is their dot product.
q′·k′ = qᵀ · R(mθ)ᵀ · R(nθ) · k
3
Rule A: turning back undoes a turn. Rule B: turns add up.
R(mθ)ᵀ = R(−mθ) ⇒ R(−mθ)·R(nθ) = R((n−m)θ)
4
So m and n never appear alone, only their difference.
score = qᵀ · R((n−m)θ) · k
Rotate by absolute position, and attention automatically sees relative distance.The pair spins together; the angle between never changes
09 · Real vectorsRoPE · Explained

Real vectors hold hundreds of numbers.RoPE pairs them up. Each pair is a dial.

12 · Inside the modelRoPE · Explained

RoPE touches Queries and Keys only.And it is re-applied in every attention layer.

Q and K get rotated

Position decides who attends to whom, and that is exactly the Q·K score.

V is left alone

Values carry what gets passed along. Pure content, no rotation needed.

Every layer, fresh

Nothing is added at the input. Each layer rotates its own Q and K, so position never fades.

Embeddings stay pure meaning. Position appears only where words are compared.Press → to highlight each part

14 · In codeRoPE · Explained

The whole idea in about 15 lines.PyTorch, applied to queries and keys.

1

Build the angles

One speed per pair, times every position: a table of angles.

2

Turn each pair

Split into (x₁, x₂) pairs and apply the 2D rotation formula.

3

Queries and keys only

Values are never rotated.

4

Ordinary attention

The usual scaled dot product, now aware of distance.

This pairs neighbours (0,1), (2,3) as in the paper. Many LLaMA ports pair the first half with the second half: same idea, different layout.Su et al., 2021

Transformers see words as a set, so they cannot tell order.
